ML PRICE ESTIMATE

RANDOM FOREST

Why preprocessing matters

The deployed application reuses the saved preprocessing pipeline from training rather than manually recreating the transformations in Django.

Training: data → preprocessing → model

Prediction: user input → saved preprocessing → model

MARKET AI ESTIMATE

Gemini + Market Research

GEMINI + SEARCH

Market AI Estimate is an AI-assisted market research pipeline. Instead of relying only on historical training data, it analyzes current Indian used-car market evidence.

MODEL B PIPELINE

How the market estimate is produced

01 User Input Same vehicle details supplied to the AI pipeline
→
02 Gemini AI AI analyzes the vehicle and market context
→
03 Market Evidence Current web and search information supports the analysis
→
04 Market AI Price Market-informed estimated vehicle value

Swipe or scroll horizontally to explore each stage.

When the primary search path is unavailable, the project can use Tavily to obtain current market evidence for the MARKET AI ESTIMATE analysis.

What MARKET AI ESTIMATE looks at

  • Vehicle identity and specifications
  • Current used-car market evidence
  • Comparable market listings
  • Market context
  • Evidence gathered from search

What it produces

  • Estimated market price
  • Confidence information
  • Search method
  • Market reasoning
  • Supporting evidence / sources

AIML LEARNING

Why use two approaches?

ML Price Estimate demonstrates supervised Machine Learning using a trained regression model. MARKET AI ESTIMATE demonstrates how an AI system can combine language-model reasoning with current market evidence.

ML Price Estimate Learns patterns from historical training data.
MARKET AI ESTIMATE Uses AI reasoning and current market evidence.
Comparison Shows how historical ML estimation differs from market-informed valuation.
